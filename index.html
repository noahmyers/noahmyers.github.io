I study how audits are regulated and how AI is changing the work accountants do.

I'm an Assistant Professor of Accounting at Utah Valley University's Woodbury School of Business. Before academia, I worked in IT Risk Assurance at Ernst & Young and in software implementation at Squire & Company. I hold a Ph.D. from Arizona State University and am a licensed CPA in Utah.
