Signature Talks

Auditable AI: Practical, Hands-On AI for Audit and Assurance

A live walk-through of AI on a realistic fraud case, in three levels: spreadsheet formulas, risk scoring, and an AI investigator working a hotline tip. Attendees see how to get useful work from AI and keep it reviewable.

FTX Unchecked: The Internal Control Failures Behind the Collapse

How weak processes, informal systems, and leadership failures compounded at FTX, and what basic controls, reconciliations, access, and documentation should look like in a fast-moving organization. Accessible to non-auditors.

Both talks run as a keynote, a CPE session, or a hands-on workshop. I also speak on data analytics, IT governance and controls, and accounting education.
